## App Review notes

Who's First? is a focused, offline group utility. To test it, place two or more fingers on the iPhone screen simultaneously and keep them down. The app waits briefly for late participants, runs an accelerating visual and native-haptic countdown, then selects one active finger using on-device randomness. Lift every finger and place them again for a new round.

No login, network connection, permissions, purchases, advertising, or user data are involved. A physical multi-touch iPhone is required to experience the core interaction and Taptic feedback.
