Who's First?

Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

Who's First? does not collect your data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What the app processes

In Together, finger locations, temporary pointer identifiers, and ring colors are processed in memory so the app can draw a ring beneath each finger and choose one at random.

In Tap In, each tap creates a temporary numbered entry with a color, tap position, and internal entry identifier. The number does not identify a person, and the app never asks for a name or contact information. Tap In entries exist only in the running app for the current group. Undo removes the newest entry; Clear and New group remove the group; reloading or closing the app removes everything.

None of this temporary interaction data is written to persistent storage or transmitted.

On-device features

Random selection, numbered-entry management, animation, sound, and haptic feedback happen entirely on your device. The native iOS app uses bundled files and does not contact the developer or any third-party service.

Data sharing and retention

Because the app does not collect data, it has nothing to share, sell, retain, or delete. Who's First? does not use advertising identifiers or track activity across other apps or websites.

Website hosting

The browser version and this public policy are hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit them in a browser, GitHub may process ordinary network and server-log information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. That hosting activity is separate from the bundled native iOS app.

Questions

For privacy questions, email contact@foliohtml.com or use the Who's First? support page.
