Who's First?

Help & Support

A tiny app for one fair decision: who goes first?

Choose Together

Every fresh launch starts in Together.

  1. Put the phone on a stable surface.
  2. Have each person use one finger to touch the iPhone.
  3. Keep every finger down while the rings pulse.
  4. The final glowing ring marks the person who goes first.

Choose with Tap In

  1. Tap the small mode icon in the upper-left. It changes to a 1–2–3 trail for Tap In.
  2. Each player taps the screen once. The app adds a numbered entry for each tap.
  3. When everyone's in, tap Pick from N. Pick becomes available after at least two entries are ready.
  4. After the one-second countdown, the glowing number goes first.

Tap In supports up to 50 entries. Tapped twice? Use Undo to remove the newest entry; its number will be reused. Clear asks before removing the entire group.

Choose again

In Together, lift every finger and place them down again. The first new touch clears the previous result and starts the next group.

In Tap In, Pick again chooses from the same numbered group, and the same number can fairly win again. New group clears every entry and leaves the app in Tap In for a different group.

Haptics and sound

The native iPhone version uses the iPhone Taptic Engine. The browser version uses a short sound where iOS does not provide web haptics. Make sure system haptics and volume are enabled if you do not feel or hear feedback.

Something not working?

Together needs at least two simultaneous touches; a mouse or trackpad cannot reproduce that experience. Lift all fingers, return to the chooser, and try again.

In Tap In, make sure at least two numbered entries are visible and finish the current tap before choosing. Opening Help & About keeps committed entries. Leaving the app during a countdown cancels that countdown so you can pick again when you return.
