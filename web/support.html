Who's First?

Help & Support

A tiny app for one fair decision: who goes first?

How to choose

  1. Put the phone on a stable surface.
  2. Have two or more people place one finger on the screen.
  3. Keep every finger down while the rings pulse.
  4. The final glowing ring marks the person who goes first.

Choose again

After the result, lift every finger and place them down again. The first new touch clears the previous result and starts the next group.

Haptics and sound

The native iPhone version uses the iPhone Taptic Engine. The browser version uses a short sound where iOS does not provide web haptics. Make sure system haptics and volume are enabled if you do not feel or hear feedback.

Something not working?

Lift all fingers, return to the chooser, and try again. Who's First? needs at least two simultaneous touches; a mouse or trackpad cannot reproduce the experience.
