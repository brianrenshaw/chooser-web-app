Three ways to choose

Find your first player.

Pick a mode from the top-left menu, gather your people, and let the app decide.

01 · Fingers down

Chooser

Everyone holds a finger on the screen. Feel the buildup, then watch one ring take the spotlight.

02 · Take your turn

Tap In

Each person taps once to join. When everyone’s in, pick from the group. Room for up to 50 players.

03 · Give it a flick

Pinball

Take your seats around the phone, flick the ball, and follow the bounces. Every seat has an equal chance.

Eight visual worlds

A different feel
for every table.

Soft watercolor, jewel colors, warm parchment, and more. Each world changes the board and its pieces together.

Choose a world in Settings, or tap with three fingers on a safe screen to shuffle colors. With VoiceOver, use the Shuffle colors action.

Made for being together

A fair choice.
Then back to the game.

Works offline, with tactile feedback and support for VoiceOver, larger text, and reduced motion. Your groups and results stay on your phone.
